Tayino - Happy Reader

快樂小讀者 · Interactive Multilingual Story Reader

About This Application & Purpose

Tayino - Happy Reader is a free, no-login-required interactive multilingual story reading web app designed for children and families. Users can immediately read stories without signing in.

  • 4 Languages & Script Switching: Supports Cantonese, Taiwanese Mandarin, Putonghua, and English with Traditional/Simplified Chinese switching and word-by-word highlighting.
  • Family Co-Reading Recordings: Parents and older siblings can record custom read-along voices stored locally on the device by default.
  • Optional Google Drive Backup: Parents can optionally connect Google Drive (drive.file scope) solely to back up and sync their own family recordings and reading progress across devices.

繁體中文簡介：快樂小讀者（Tayino - Happy Reader）專為多孩家庭設計，免註冊登入即可直接開始閱讀，支援粵語、國語、普通話、英語四語點讀與逐詞高亮；父母與兄姊可錄製專屬陪讀音頻保存於本機，並可選透過 Google Drive 跨設備備份家庭錄音。
