Privacy Policy — Tayino - Happy Reader

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 | Domain: https://tayino.com

Tayino - Happy Reader ("we", "our", or "the Application"), operated at https://tayino.com, is an interactive multilingual story-reading web application designed for families and children. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, protect, and share user data, specifically regarding Google User Data accessed via Google OAuth 2.0 and Google Drive APIs.

1. Data Collection & Local-First Architecture

  • No Mandatory Account Registration: Users can access and use the core reading features of Tayino - Happy Reader without creating an account or signing in.
  • Local Device Storage: Reading preferences, family member profiles, and read-along audio recordings are stored locally on your device (IndexedDB / LocalStorage) by default and are never automatically transmitted to our servers.
  • Optional Google Account & Google Drive Data: Only when a parent or adult guardian explicitly clicks to connect Google Drive for cloud backup, we request OAuth 2.0 authorization for:
    • openid, userinfo.email, and userinfo.profile: To identify the authorized Google account and display account connection status within the app.
    • https://googleapis.com: Limited strictly to creating and managing a dedicated backup folder ("Tayino Happy Reader Backup") and audio/progress files created by this Application. We cannot view, read, modify, or delete any other files in your Google Drive.

2. How We Use Google User Data

Google User Data accessed by Tayino - Happy Reader is used exclusively for the following user-facing functionality:

  • To back up, synchronize, and restore family read-along voice recordings and story reading progress across devices owned by the same family;
  • To display the connected Google account email address in the parent settings panel so users know which cloud account is active.

3. Data Storage, Security & Retention

  • No Server-Side Audio Storage: We do not upload, store, listen to, or analyze your family voice recordings on our own backend servers. Data transfers occur directly between your browser and Google's official API endpoints over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.2+).
  • OAuth Token Handling: OAuth access tokens are held transiently in browser session memory solely to execute user-initiated sync operations and are never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Data Retention & Deletion: Backup files remain in your personal Google Drive until you delete them. You can delete local data anytime in the app, delete the backup folder in Google Drive, or revoke Tayino - Happy Reader's access at Google Account Permissions.

4. Limited Use Disclosure & No Data Sharing

  • Google API Services User Data Policy: Tayino - Happy Reader's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Zero Third-Party Sharing or Advertising: We never sell, rent, trade, or transfer Google user data or children's voice recordings to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.

5. Children's Privacy & Parental Control

Tayino - Happy Reader is built for parent-child co-reading. Cloud synchronization and Google OAuth sign-in are strictly optional features intended to be initiated and managed by parents or adult guardians.

6. 中文隱私權政策摘要（Traditional Chinese Summary）

Tayino - Happy Reader（快樂小讀者）預設免註冊登入，所有閱讀設定與兒童陪讀錄音僅保存於本機瀏覽器。僅在家長主動啟用「Google Drive 雲端備份」時，本應用才會透過 drive.file 最小權限在您的個人 Google Drive 建立專屬備份資料夾（無法存取您的其他雲端檔案）。我們絕不在自有伺服器留存、出售或向任何第三方分享您的錄音與 Google 帳號資料，並嚴格遵守《Google API 服務用戶數據政策》的「有限使用」規範。
